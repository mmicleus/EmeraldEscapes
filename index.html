Our Story

Emerald Escapes was founded in 2011 in Dingle, County Kerry, by Siobhán and Declan Murphy, a former tour guide and a retired ferry captain. Tired of watching visitors rush through Ireland on packed coach schedules, they set out to show the country they knew: the hidden music sessions, the family-run guesthouses and the places no guidebook mentions. Today our team of 40 travel designers and local guides creates journeys across all 32 counties, always through the people who live here.

We design trips for couples after a long weekend, families looking for easy days out, overseas visitors tracing their roots, and groups of friends who want something outdoors.
