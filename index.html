Our Story

Emerald Escapes began in 2015 when two friends from Galway got tired of watching visitors queue at the same five attractions. We now plan holidays and short breaks across all 32 counties, with a focus on places and hosts that most itineraries skip.

We design trips for couples after a long weekend, families looking for easy days out, overseas visitors tracing their roots, and groups of friends who want something outdoors.
